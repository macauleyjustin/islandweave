Resilient Local Communications Ready

This site is hosted directly from a peer's local IWP-OBJECT chunk store, cryptographically signed with an IWP-SERVICE record, and browsable even when upstream internet connections are disconnected.

How Verification Works

Every asset (HTML, CSS, images, and documents) is split into SHA-256 content-addressed chunks and verified against a signed manifest before rendering inside IslandWeave Navigator.
